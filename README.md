# Ohms_Law_Sim
The **Ohm’s Law & Specific Resistance Practical Simulator** is an interactive virtual physics laboratory designed to replicate real-world electrical and material measurement experiments directly in the browser. In the first module, users construct and operate a complete Ohm's Law circuit featuring a rheostat, selectable power supplies, and damped moving-coil voltmeters and ammeters—complete with realistic meter loading effects, zero-error calibration, and zoomable analog dials—to collect $V$–$I$ data and automatically plot a best-fit linear regression graph. The second module transitions to a virtual Wire Lab where users measure the unknown resistance wire's physical dimensions using an interactive micrometer screw gauge (with ratchet locking, pitch/division controls, and zero-error correction) and a magnified metre scale, ultimately combining their experimental resistance, diameter, and length to calculate the wire's specific resistance ($\rho$) and identify its material composition.
